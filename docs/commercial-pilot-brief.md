# Kenya Micro-Retail Pulse — validation brief

Status: discovery artifact only. No buyer interview, design-partner commitment, paid pilot, or market-demand claim has been recorded yet.

This brief is the bounded experiment required before building a self-service commercial-data product. It does not enable commercial collection; the current backend policy remains disabled by default.

## Hypothesis

Consumer-goods brands and distributors may pay for a recurring, quality-disclosed view of observed category demand, price movement, stockouts and payment mix in Kenyan micro-retail. The report must describe participating-network coverage, not total market share.

## First pilot offer

Kenya Micro-Retail Pulse, one category and one broad region, delivered as a manually quality-checked weekly or monthly report. Candidate metrics are:

- demand index and period-over-period movement;
- observed price bands and changes;
- stockout rate where stock coverage supports it;
- payment-method mix;
- coverage, freshness, suppression and quality notes.

Initial pricing bands are hypotheses to test, not market facts: KSh 25,000–75,000 per month for a narrow pilot report, or a separately scoped fixed-price research brief.

## Interview evidence to collect

For each prospective buyer, record:

1. Segment and role (brand/distributor, wholesaler, payments provider, researcher/NGO, or financial institution).
2. One recurring decision currently made with poor or delayed data.
3. How often the decision is made and the cost of being wrong.
4. Current data source, workaround and spend.
5. The minimum category, geographic resolution, freshness and coverage needed.
6. Budget owner and procurement path.
7. Which sample output would change the decision.
8. Objections about methodology, privacy, licensing or reliability.
9. Price reaction to the narrow pilot and what commitment is possible.

Do not show real contribution rows, shop names, contacts, receipts, precise locations, partner references or any other restricted source data. Use synthetic fixtures or approved aggregate examples only.

## Decision gate

Do not build advanced forecasting, a warehouse, a public self-service portal or a partner API until the following evidence exists:

- 15–20 structured interviews across at least three buyer segments;
- at least two written design-partner commitments;
- one paid pilot sought and its outcome recorded;
- a single repeated report request with a defined buyer decision;
- delivery cost, gross contribution and servicing effort measured for the pilot;
- coverage, freshness, suppression and legal/privacy disclosures accepted by the buyer;
- an explicit decision to proceed, revise the offer, or abandon it.
